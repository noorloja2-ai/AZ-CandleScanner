AZ CandleScanner v16.11

- Added ENABLE/DISABLE BANNER MONITOR with saved state.
- Closing the banner disables only the banner; signal notifications remain active.
- Banner now shows NEXT CANDLE BUY, SELL or NO TRADE.
- Added confidence score, candlestick pattern, market condition, correct detected pair/timeframe and entry time.
- Poor, weak, mixed or below-threshold conditions now show NO TRADE without a misleading high percentage.
- When no named candlestick pattern is confirmed, the banner shows PATTERN: NOT DETECTED while market direction continues to be calculated.
- Existing learning, notification and draggable-position behavior is preserved.
