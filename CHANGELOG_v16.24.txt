AZ CandleScanner v16.24

- Medium Chance is removed from the entire app.
- Safer Mode and Pattern Mode both require at least 85% for BUY or SELL.
- 85-89% is HIGH CHANCE and 90-100% is VERY HIGH CONFIDENCE.
- Every setup below 85% shows NO TRADE and creates no popup or Android notification.
- The Controls page cannot set signal or sound thresholds below 85%.
- Keeps persistent notifications, automatic update checks and all existing learning data.
- Android still requires user approval before installing a downloaded APK update.
