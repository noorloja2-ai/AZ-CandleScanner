AZ CandleScanner v16.25

- Local calibration activates only after 30 exact-close outcomes for the pair and timeframe.
- Pattern quality is learned separately for each pair, timeframe and named setup.
- Pattern quality affects decisions only after 30 verified outcomes in that exact segment.
- Recent verified outcomes receive a small bounded weight while older knowledge is retained.
- Market drift is detected when the last 30 verified outcomes fall below 50% accuracy.
- Automatic rollback restores the last healthy calibration checkpoint.
- Training page shows learning lock, drift and rollback status.
- Medium Chance remains removed; all BUY/SELL signals require at least 85%.
- Preserves existing local samples, community learning and v16.24 behaviour.
