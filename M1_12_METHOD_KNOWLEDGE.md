# AZ CandleScanner — 12-Method M1 Confluence

The next completed one-minute candle is classified as BUY, SELL, or WAIT by
combining twelve context families:

1. HH/HL or LH/LL market structure
2. Support/resistance and role reversal
3. Confirmed candlestick pattern
4. Candle body, wick, rejection, and closing-position price action
5. Confirmed breakout/retest or failed breakout
6. Liquidity sweep and FVG context
7. BOS/CHoCH-style confirmation
8. EMA 9/21/50/200 trend context proxy
9. RSI-14 momentum/exhaustion context proxy
10. Fibonacci 50/61.8/78.6 confluence proxy
11. Recent versus broader timeframe agreement
12. Validated asset/timeframe learning

## Decision rules

- Every family contributes at most one independent confirmation.
- A named pattern cannot create a trade by itself.
- High-accuracy mode accepts the 12-method override only with at least 7/12
  confirmations and a strong directional score.
- Normal directional agreement begins at 5/12 confirmations.
- Conflicting, weak, excessively volatile, doji-like, or low-quality states
  remain WAIT.
- Indicator values inferred from screen pixels are context proxies, not exact
  broker indicator readings.
- The displayed score is confluence strength, not a guaranteed win percentage.
- Learning is updated only after the corresponding future candle closes.

Existing Candlestick Bible knowledge, uploaded-book knowledge, broker-board
learning, local learning, community-learning sync, and notification behaviour
remain enabled.
