CandleScanner v15.3 — Full Notification Information

- Tap a BUY / SELL notification to reopen the complete signal card.
- The expanded notification includes asset, timeframe, trade duration, entry time,
  entry status, setup, buyer/seller pressure and recent win-rate information.
- Notifications remain visible until CLOSE is pressed.
- CLOSE removes both the signal card and its notification.
- Keeps AZ tap scanning, HIGH CHANCE / MOST SURE safety filters and self-learning.

Signals are estimates, not guarantees. The app does not place trades automatically.
