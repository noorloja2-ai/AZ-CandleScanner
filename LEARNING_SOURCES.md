## Important design rule

The uploaded sources repeatedly emphasize confirmation and context. v14.2 therefore treats named patterns, FVG/liquidity labels and indicator crosses as confluence rather than guaranteed BUY/SELL triggers. Screenshot-derived geometry is approximate and confidence is a model score, not a guaranteed probability.
