AZ CandleScanner v16.18

- Adds an Automatic Pattern BUY / SELL switch in Controls.
- The switch is OFF by default, preserving the safer v16.16 confirmation logic.
- Safe Mode keeps its existing local and shared learner; experimental pattern-mode outcomes are stored separately and cannot change Safe Mode calibration.
- A confirmed bullish pattern automatically creates BUY.
- A confirmed bearish pattern automatically creates SELL.
- Neutral, unconfirmed and indecision patterns remain NO TRADE.
- POOR/GOOD/BEST market condition remains visible as a warning and no longer blocks a directional pattern signal.
